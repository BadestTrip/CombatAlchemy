# Brewing Feedback and Independent Fire

Implemented direction:

- Preserve two-release scoring and quality multipliers.
- Give every release immediate flask-local visual and SFX feedback.
- Give total quality `+2` a persistent bottle accent and completion cue.
- Let the player toggle fire independently; valid brewing auto-lights it only
  when needed and restores its previous state unless manually overridden.
- Extinguished fire pauses height growth without cancelling the brew.
- Above 90% power, enlarge the flame and add a localized gold glow.
- Keep simulation, controller policy, presentation, audio, and tuning modular.
